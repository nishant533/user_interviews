Pain point prioritisation table with problem statements for merged P2 and P3 segment

# Pain point Segment Frequency Impact Problem statement Rationale
1
No role-specific reference point
"I don't know what AI to do for my job"
 P2 + P3 Very high Very high
Professionals can't start using AI meaningfully because they've never seen someone in their exact role do it.
 ~30% of survey named this as primary barrier. Most upstream blocker — nothing else matters until this is solved.
2
The decomposition wall
Can't describe what to automate in buildable terms
 P2 + P3 Very high Very high
Professionals know they want to automate something but can't name the trigger, steps, or output — so they never start.
 Confirmed across every non-technical stuck profile. Blocker exists before any tool is even opened.
3
No same-role peer proof
Cross-role demos inspire but don't activate
 P2 + P3 High Very high
Seeing AI used by someone in a different role doesn't translate — people need to see their exact job being done with AI to act.
 Strongest finding in the research. Every confirmed activation traced to same-role peer demo, never to content.
4
No first win in session one
Learning feels disconnected from real work
 Mostly P2 High Medium
Without a visible output in the first session, users deprioritise and drop off before AI becomes a habit.
 Downstream of #1–3. Solving upstream blockers likely produces this naturally.
5
Plateau without realising it
Daily usage identical to week one
 Mostly P2 Medium Medium
Users think they're using AI well because they use it daily — but their usage hasn't grown and they don't know what they're missing.
 Hard to solve because users don't feel the pain — low urgency on their end makes it a weak product hook.
6
Time and firefighting
No margin to sit down and build
 Mostly P3 Medium Medium
Motivated users who know what they want to build can't find the time or structure to actually do it in their workday.
 Mechanism is missing, not motivation. Fixing upstream blockers reduces the time cost significantly.
7
Domain trust failure
Professional risk of wrong outputs
 Finance only Low High
In high-stakes domains, users won't act until they're confident AI outputs are accurate and safe enough to use professionally.
 One data point. Likely a separate ICP, not a general P2-new problem.
